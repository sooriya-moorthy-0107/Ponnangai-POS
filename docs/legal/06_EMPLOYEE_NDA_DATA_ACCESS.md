## RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**

A. The Employer operates a housekeeping product manufacturing and retail business under the brand "Ponnangai Enterprises", selling products including but not limited to: cleaning liquids, fabric care, kitchen care, floor care, hygiene products, and surface care solutions.

B. The Employer has developed and deployed a proprietary **Point of Sale (POS) system** ("Ponnangai POS") and a **corporate website** for its business operations.

C. The Employee has been or will be given access to the Company's confidential information, trade secrets, business data, and/or the POS system in the course of their employment.

D. The parties wish to protect the Company's confidential information and intellectual property.

## AGREEMENT

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Employee's employment and the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information, whether written, oral, electronic, or visual, relating to the Company's business, operations, products, systems, and strategies. This includes, but is not limited to:

#### 1.1 Business & Financial Data

- Sales figures, revenue data, and daily/monthly sales reports
- Product pricing, cost structures, profit margins, and discount policies
- Cash transaction records (Cash IN/OUT logs)
- Customer lists, wholesale client details, and institutional buyer contacts
- Supplier information, raw material costs, and vendor agreements
- Business strategies, expansion plans, and market intelligence

#### 1.2 POS System & Technology

- The source code, architecture, logic, and design of the Ponnangai POS system
- Database schemas, API endpoints, and system configurations
- User credentials, passwords, and access tokens
- Session management mechanisms, security configurations, and encryption keys
- Receipt templates, report generation logic, and analytics algorithms
- Bluetooth printer integration protocols and ESC/POS command sequences
- CSV import/export templates and data processing workflows

#### 1.3 Product Information

- Product formulations, recipes, and manufacturing processes
- Ingredient lists, chemical compositions, and quality control parameters
- Packaging specifications and branding guidelines
- Product images, labels, and marketing materials
- Unreleased or in-development product lines

#### 1.4 Operational Data

- Inventory levels, stock counts, and supply chain logistics
- Employee information, compensation details, and organisational structure
- Internal policies, SOPs, and training materials
- Bill and transaction histories accessible through the POS system

---

### 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE EMPLOYEE

The Employee agrees to:

#### 2.1 Non-Disclosure

1. **Not disclose** any Confidential Information to any third party — including competitors, friends, family, or the public — without the prior written consent of the Employer.
2. **Not share** POS system login credentials with any unauthorised person.
3. **Not discuss** sales data, pricing information, or business strategies outside the workplace.
4. **Not post** screenshots, photos, or descriptions of the POS system, internal dashboards, or reports on social media or any public platform.

#### 2.2 Non-Use

1. **Not use** any Confidential Information for personal benefit or for the benefit of any other business or individual.
2. **Not copy, reproduce, or extract** data from the POS system, sales reports, or business records for personal use.
3. **Not reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract** the source code or logic of the POS system.

#### 2.3 Data Access

1. Access the POS system **only through the assigned username and role** provided by the Employer.
2. **Not attempt** to access features, data, or reports beyond the permissions assigned to the Employee's role (Owner > Admin > Manager > Shopkeeper / Factory).
3. **Not share** or transfer POS access credentials to any other person, even within the organisation, without Employer authorisation.
4. **Report immediately** any suspected security breach, unauthorized access, or data leak to the Employer.

#### 2.4 Return of Materials

Upon termination of employment (whether voluntary or involuntary), the Employee shall:

1. **Immediately return** all Company property, including devices, documents, product samples, and printed materials.
2. **Delete** any Company data stored on personal devices (phone, laptop, USB drives, cloud storage).
3. **Not retain** any copies, backups, or extracts of Confidential Information in any form.

---

### 3. POS SYSTEM ACCESS RULES

The Employee acknowledges and agrees to the following POS system access rules based on role assignment:

| Role | Permitted Actions | Restricted Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopkeeper** | View own inventory, process sales, print receipts, view own bill history | Cannot access other shops' data, admin panel, reports, or user management |
| **Factory** | Process factory sales, view factory bills, no stock deduction | Cannot access admin panel, reports, or manage users |
| **Manager** | View reports, manage inventory & products, create Shopkeeper accounts | Cannot delete users, cannot change passwords, cannot access Owner settings |
| **Admin** | Full system access, user management, password resets, analytics | Subject to audit by Owner |
| **Owner** | Complete unrestricted access | N/A |

Violation of role-based access restrictions will be treated as a **serious breach** of this Agreement.

---

### 4. DURATION OF OBLIGATIONS

#### 4.1 During Employment
All obligations under this Agreement are in full force during the entire period of the Employee's employment with Ponnangai Enterprises.

#### 4.2 After Termination
The non-disclosure obligations under Section 2 shall **survive for a period of 2 (two) years** after the termination of the Employee's employment, regardless of the reason for termination.

#### 4.3 Trade Secrets
Obligations regarding **trade secrets** (product formulations, POS source code, proprietary algorithms) shall continue **indefinitely**, for as long as such information remains a trade secret.

---

### 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

1. Any work created by the Employee during the course of employment, using Company resources, or relating to Company business, shall be the **exclusive intellectual property of Ponnangai Enterprises**.
2. The Employee assigns all rights, title, and interest in any such work to the Employer.
3. The Employee shall not claim ownership of any modifications, improvements, or derivative works created for the Company.

---

### 6. CONSEQUENCES OF BREACH

Any breach of this Agreement may result in:

1. **Disciplinary Action**: Including warning, suspension, or termination of employment.
2. **Legal Action**: The Employer reserves the right to pursue civil and criminal remedies under:
   - The Indian Contract Act, 1872 (breach of contract)
   - The Information Technology Act, 2000 (unauthorised access, data theft — Sections 43, 65, 66)
   - The Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (copyright infringement)
   - Any other applicable laws
3. **Injunctive Relief**: The Employer may seek an immediate injunction from a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent further disclosure or use of Confidential Information.
4. **Damages**: The Employee shall be liable for all losses, damages, and costs (including legal fees) suffered by the Employer as a result of the breach.

---

### 7. EXCEPTIONS

This Agreement does not apply to information that:

1. Is or becomes **publicly available** through no fault of the Employee.
2. Was known to the Employee **prior to employment** (with documentary evidence).
3. Is **independently developed** by the Employee without use of Confidential Information.
4. Is disclosed pursuant to a **court order or legal requirement** — provided the Employee notifies the Employer promptly.

---

### 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### 8.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior agreements on this subject.

#### 8.2 Severability
If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

#### 8.3 Amendment
No modification of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

#### 8.4 Governing Law & Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the **exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India**.
