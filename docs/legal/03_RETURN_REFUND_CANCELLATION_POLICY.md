## 2. RETURN ELIGIBILITY

### 2.1 Eligible Returns

You may request a return if:

1. **Defective or Damaged Product**: The product was received in a damaged, leaking, or defective condition.
2. **Wrong Product Delivered**: You received a product different from what was ordered.
3. **Quality Issue**: The product has a manufacturing defect or does not meet stated product specifications.
4. **Expired Product**: The product was delivered past its usable shelf life (if applicable).

### 2.2 Return Window

| Order Type | Return Window |
|---|---|
| **Retail (Individual Purchase)** | **7 days** from the date of purchase |
| **Wholesale / Bulk Orders** | **14 days** from the date of delivery |

Returns must be initiated within the above time frames. Requests received after the return window will not be accepted.

### 2.3 Conditions for Return

All returns are subject to the following conditions:

1. **Unopened Packaging**: The product must be in its **original, sealed, and unopened packaging**. Products with broken seals, tampered caps, or opened containers will **NOT** be accepted for return.
2. **Original Condition**: The product must be unused, undamaged (by the customer), and in the same condition as received.
3. **Proof of Purchase**: You must present a valid **receipt (bill)** or proof of purchase. Our POS-generated receipts include a Bill Number, Cashier Name, Date/Time, and itemised list — any of these may be used as verification.
4. **Return Packaging**: Products should be returned in their original packaging, with all labels, tags, and accessories intact.

### 2.4 Non-Returnable Items

The following products/situations are **NOT eligible** for return:

1. Products that have been **opened, partially used, or consumed**.
2. Products with **customer-inflicted damage** (e.g., container dropped, spillage due to improper storage).
3. Products purchased more than **7 days (retail) or 14 days (wholesale)** ago.
4. Products purchased during a **clearance sale or promotional offer**, unless defective.
5. Products returned without a valid **receipt or proof of purchase**.
6. Returns initiated due to **change of mind** or personal preference (e.g., fragrance not liked).

## 4. REFUND POLICY

### 4.1 Refund Methods

| Payment Mode (Original Purchase) | Refund Method |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | Cash refund at the retail outlet, or bank transfer (at our discretion) |
| **UPI** | Refund to the same UPI ID within 5–7 business days |
| **Card** | Refund to the original card within 7–10 business days (subject to bank processing) |

### 4.2 Refund Amount

- **Full Refund**: Issued for defective, damaged, wrong, or expired products that pass our inspection.
- **Partial Refund**: May be issued if the product shows signs of partial use or minor damage not caused by us.
- **No Refund**: For products that fail inspection or do not meet return eligibility criteria.

### 4.3 Refund Timeline

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Return Request Acknowledgement | Within 2 business days |
| Product Inspection | Within 3 business days of receipt |
| Refund Processing | Within 5–7 business days after approval |
| Bank/UPI Credit Reflection | 2–5 additional banking days (varies by bank) |

**Total estimated time from return initiation to refund receipt: 10–15 business days.**

### 4.4 Shipping Costs for Returns

- If the return is due to **our error** (defective, wrong product, damaged in transit), we will bear the return shipping costs.
- If the return is initiated for **any other reason** (where accepted as an exception), the customer bears the return shipping cost.

## 5. ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY

### 5.1 Before Dispatch
If your order has **not yet been dispatched**, you may cancel it by contacting us immediately via WhatsApp or phone. A full refund will be issued for prepaid orders.

### 5.2 After Dispatch
Once an order has been dispatched:
- **Cancellation is not possible**. Please wait to receive the order and then follow the Return Process (Section 3) if applicable.
- In exceptional circumstances (e.g., duplicate order), contact us immediately and we will evaluate on a case-by-case basis.

### 5.3 Cancellation by Ponnangai Enterprises
We reserve the right to cancel any order at our sole discretion, including:
- If the ordered product is out of stock or discontinued.
- If pricing errors are identified after the order is placed.
- If we suspect fraudulent or malicious activity.
- For non-payment or payment disputes.

In such cases, a full refund will be issued for any amount already paid.

## 6. EXCHANGE POLICY

Instead of a refund, you may opt for a **like-for-like product exchange** subject to:
1. The product meeting all return conditions in Section 2.3.
2. The replacement product being available in stock.
3. Exchanges completed within the return window.

For exchanges, no additional charges will be applied if the replacement product is of equal or lesser value. For products of higher value, the price difference must be paid at the time of exchange.
