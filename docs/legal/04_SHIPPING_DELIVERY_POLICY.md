## 8. DELIVERY INSTRUCTIONS

### 8.1 Receiving Delivery

- Please ensure someone is available at the delivery address during the estimated delivery window.
- Verify the product condition and packaging at the time of delivery.
- If the package appears **tampered, damaged, or leaking**, please note it on the delivery receipt and contact us immediately (within 24 hours).

### 8.2 Failed Delivery Attempts

- If delivery is attempted and the recipient is unavailable, the courier will make **up to 2 additional attempts**.
- After 3 failed attempts, the package will be **returned to sender** (our warehouse).
- Re-dispatch charges may apply for returned packages.

---

## 9. DAMAGE IN TRANSIT

If you receive a product that is **damaged, leaking, or broken during transit**:

1. **Do not use** the product.
2. **Photograph** the damage (outer packaging and product).
3. Contact us within **24 hours** of delivery via WhatsApp (+91 7092148969) or email.
4. We will arrange a **free replacement or full refund** as per our [Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy](./03_RETURN_REFUND_CANCELLATION_POLICY.md).
